The display title is editable now: Settings -> Card to card -> "Display title" (c2c_title, default "کارت به کارت", up to 60 characters). It is used for the checkout option, the transfer dialog header, the admin Telegram request message, the buyer's approval/rejection Telegram message, the bot's /start text, and the payment-method label written on the order (shortened to fit the column). Nothing is hard-coded to "کارت به کارت" on the buyer's side any more.
