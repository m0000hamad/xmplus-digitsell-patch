The wallet now sits where operator apps put it. The balance tile in the
subscription card shows the wallet balance with "افزایش موجودی"; everything
else - adding funds, transactions by day, server prices, the "continue on my
balance" switch - opens in a side panel (a bottom sheet on phones), also from
the new "کیف پول" menu item. While an account runs on its wallet, the
subscription card says so instead of showing "1 day left". Billing messages
appear as a short notice at the top of the page.

Admin: the wallet amounts are in toman, the minimum top-up can be anything
including 0 (no minimum), and saving shows exactly what was stored.

Also in this release: the status rows in Happ / V2Box / v2rayNG / Shadowrocket
(end date in the Solar Hijri calendar, data left, wallet balance) - switch
them on under the same settings after trying the link in an app.
