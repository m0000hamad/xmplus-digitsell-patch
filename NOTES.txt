Fix: a card's cap now counts only approved payments, so abandoned or still-pending test requests no longer keep a card "full" and hide card to card from the checkout. The amount cap is in rial and the admin hint now says so (45000000 for 45 million), which matters because a small number left in the amount field fills the card at once. Also: the support handle (@digitsellshop by default, editable in Settings -> Card to card) now appears in the buyer's approval and rejection Telegram message, in the rejection reason shown in the panel banner, and in the checkout dialog; the bot token field, the webhook status and the dedicated-bot setup from 1.28.0 are unchanged.
