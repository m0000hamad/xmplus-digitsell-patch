Exit location per server: a new card on the Servers page picks the country
each Xray server's customers come out of (through Tor). The server's own
digitsell-xray node does everything by itself within a minute - installs
tor-geo, starts Tor for that country, and switches over only once it works.

node: run "digitsell-xray update" there.
