The admin dashboard is rebuilt, and the servers page shows how much traffic
each server carries.

Dashboard: every number comes from one read-only admin call,
xmplus-patch.php?do=dashboard.overview. Headline cards with trend lines and
the change against the previous period; daily revenue split into
subscription, top-up and time; a 12-month trend (Solar Hijri months in
Persian); the sales mix with first purchase against renewal, and the
gateways; top plans; the busiest weekday and hour; users by status; who
expires over the next 14 days; sign-ups; traffic by day and by server; the
nodes; the latest paid orders.

A system health card checks the database, the nodes' heartbeats, the cron
runner, node traffic reports, the paid rate of the last 24 hours, free disk,
load, the template cache and any paid time order not yet applied.

Servers: per-server traffic for today, the week and the month, drawn as a
chart and a ranking, and written next to each server in the list.
