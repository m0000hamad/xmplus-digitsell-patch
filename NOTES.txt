New sign-in page.

view/auth/login.tpl is redesigned: a dark glass card with the Digitsell
header. On a desktop (1024 px and wider) it is a split screen - the form on
the right, the Network Optimizer features and the social icons on the left,
all on one screen. On a phone only the form shows, with the social icons at
the bottom of the card, and it fits one screen without scrolling. Inputs are
16 px on phones so iOS does not zoom on focus.

The form keeps the panel's own contract: it posts email and passwd (plus the
hCaptcha or Turnstile response when enabled) to /login and follows ret 1 to
/portal/dashboard and ret 2 to /verify. Errors show under the fields instead
of a layer popup. Social links are icons only; Telegram goes to
t.me/digitsellshop (support).

Everything is served from the panel itself - IRANSans, Inter, Font Awesome
and the compiled Tailwind CSS inlined in the template - so the page does not
depend on any outside CDN. Source and build script: tools/login/.
