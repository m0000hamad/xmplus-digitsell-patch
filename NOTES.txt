Promotions can be posted in a Telegram channel. Settings gets a "promotion
channel" card: the channel's numeric id, a save button and a test message
button. Each promotion has a "post in the channel" switch; when it is on, the
site bot posts the plan like its card on the site: the discount / special /
prize tags, list price struck through next to the promo price for every
cycle, traffic and users, the prize range, occasion, deadline and how many
are left, with a buy button. The post is edited when the promotion changes or
stock drops, and marked as ended (button removed) when the promotion closes.
