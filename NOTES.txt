Fix: the card-to-card admin section and the checkout dialog no longer rely on jQuery, which this panel loads in the footer after the inline scripts run, so at parse time `$` did not exist. Every button in Settings -> Card to card (Add card, Save, Save cards, approve/reject) was bound inside `$(function(){...})`, which never ran - the section rendered but stayed inert and the card list stayed empty. The admin manager now binds its listeners with a plain DOM-ready guard (no jQuery), and the checkout dialog opens through the Bootstrap Modal API. No other change.
