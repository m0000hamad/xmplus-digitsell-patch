A prize won with a promoted plan now shows on the site, not only on Telegram.
Back on the dashboard after paying, a gold note says the prize is being
drawn; the page keeps asking and, as soon as it lands (about a minute), the
congratulation popup shows it: +N GB or +N days, for buying which plan.

The prize also shows in the numbers, in gold: a chip on the subscription card
(+N GB / +N days prize), a gold inner ring and a "prize left" line on the
statistics card, and a note that the prize is used first - the prize figure
runs down to zero before the plan's own traffic does.
