Direct routes for Xray customers: on the OpenVPN "direct routes" card, the new
"Xray customers too (Happ)" switch sends the same list - Iranian sites and
addresses plus the custom lines - to the Happ app with the subscription link,
so those destinations open directly instead of through the server. New: an
Xray node of our own (node/xray) that replaces the XMPlus node binary with the
official, newest Xray-core; see node/xray/README.md.
