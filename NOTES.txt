Card-to-card payments, a manual method the encoded gateway list cannot hold. Settings -> Card to card manages several receiving cards, each with its own cap (maximum amount and/or number of transfers, per day, per month or in total, 0 = no cap); the checkout always shows the first enabled card in sort order that still has room and rotates to the next one automatically. The buyer's declaration is deliberately minimal: only the last four digits of their own card are required, because the bank SMS already carries the name, the time and the reference. Payer name, transfer date/time and reference sit in an optional "more details" section, pre-filled and editable; the admin Telegram message shows whatever was filled plus the submission time and everything needed to match the transfer. Two approve/reject buttons ride on that message (signed one-time links opening a confirmation page - no bot webhook is needed), and the same decision can be made in Settings -> Card to card. Approval marks the order paid and grants what it bought: time plans, reserved plans and pay-as-you-go charges are picked up by their own jobs, a normal top-up adds its gigabytes, and a subscription follows the plan's renewal type (or always starts fresh, per the admin setting). The buyer sees the result in the pay dialog immediately and gets a confirmation banner on every customer page; a rejection carries the admin's reason to the buyer's Telegram whenever they have started the bot (independently of the panel's Telegram master switch) and to that banner. A minute job keeps waiting orders from being cancelled by the panel's own timeout, auto-rejects requests nobody looked at, removes the Telegram buttons once a request is decided, and retries any notification that failed. New tables c2c_card, c2c_request and c2c_notice (migration 025), off until switched on. Manifest and VERSION to 1.27.1.
