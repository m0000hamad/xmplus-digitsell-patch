Stops per-day purchases from looking like plans, and puts a ceiling on them.

A per-day time plan still mints one package row per day count - the encoded
checkout can only price an order from a real package row, and the pay page and
order history read the name from it, so the row cannot be deleted afterwards.
What changed is that those rows no longer read as plans: the admin plans list
hides them, and opening one refuses to save it. Previously, opening a generated
row and saving turned it into a priceless "fixed" time plan, which then showed
up as a real plan - one such row on the live panel has been put back.

Two limits per time plan, both editable in the plan form and both counted per
subscription: how many times a user may buy it, and how many days they may add
in total. Zero means no limit, and the count starts again at the user's next
paid subscription order. A plan with nothing left is not offered at all; a
per-day plan with some room left narrows its own day range to what remains.
Both are enforced again server-side at mint time.
