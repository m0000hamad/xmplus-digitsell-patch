Lighter sign-in page.

The page no longer loads Font Awesome (67 KB of CSS plus about 375 KB of
webfonts) for its 17 icons: tools/login/icons.py subsets them into two tiny
fonts (about 3 KB) inlined in the template. Inter is gone (system font for the
few Latin words) and IRANSans is declared in two weights only, with
font-display: swap and the regular weight preloaded. The three animated
600 px background glows under a 130-150 px blur filter are one static
gradient, and the glass blur is lighter (12 px, none on the header and
footer bars), which is what made weaker phones stutter.

Before: about 520 KB over five requests. After: 16 KB of HTML and two
28 KB fonts.
