Fix: cancelling a paid reservation sometimes did not refund and sent no Telegram notice. The refund checked `orders.status === 1`, but the panel's own order list can move a paid order out of that status (or delete the row) between payment and cancel, while `pay_date` is the durable paid proof everyone else uses and `plan_reserve.amount` remembers what was paid. `reserve.cancel` now refunds on `pay_date` (falling back to `plan_reserve.amount` when the order row is gone), and `ReserveJob::reconcile()` (every minute) cancels, refunds and notifies any waiting reservation whose order is missing or dead (`-2/-1/0`). Manifest and VERSION to 1.26.1.
