Promotions on subscription plans. The plan form gets a promotion box: a real
percentage discount (the discounted price is what the checkout charges; the
list price is kept aside and put back when it ends), a "special" badge, and a
prize switch that gives every buyer a random amount of traffic or days from a
range, about a minute after payment, exactly once per order. Optional occasion
text, end date with a live countdown, and a sales limit with a "N left"
counter; the promotion closes itself when either runs out. Plan cards and the
plan page show animated badges, the struck-through list price and the
discount, in night and day and on phones. Buyers hear about their purchase and
prize on Telegram and by e-mail; the admin chats hear about every purchase and
every start and end.

Also: User::sendMail hands the template its values, queues a mail as JSON the
way UserJob does, and reports a failed send instead of calling it sent.
