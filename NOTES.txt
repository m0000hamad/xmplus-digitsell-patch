Announcements: the admin notices page now carries a "دو اعلان" card with two independent broadcast campaigns. Campaign 1 is the notice - the single "ارسال اطلاعیه" select on the add/edit notice form is now three separate switches (Telegram, E-mail, Group/bot); saving a notice stores its title and body as campaign 1 and queues it once if any channel is on. Campaign 2 is the dashboard invite nudge: a switch that turns the invite popup on and off (setting invite_popup, read from $Config so the popup hides on the next page load), an editable invite message, and the same three channels. Each campaign sends to Telegram, e-mail and a single group/channel post set as announce_group_id, all driven from app/Patch/Announce.php, bin/announce.php and App\Jobs\AnnounceJob (scheduled every minute): a batch per run, every customer claimed in the new announce_broadcast table first, so nobody is told twice, and customers who switched notices off are skipped. Taking over the notice send means the notice is saved with sendmail=0, so the panel's own all-in-one send no longer fires. Migration 021 adds invite_popup. Manifest and VERSION to 1.23.23.
