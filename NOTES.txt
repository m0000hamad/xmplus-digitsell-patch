OpenVPN servers on the same subscription. Customers can also connect with the
OpenVPN app: the login is checked by the panel and the traffic counts against
the same plan (data and end date) and the charge wallet; the connection is cut
when the plan or its data runs out. Settings -> OpenVPN servers adds a server
and prints the install command for it (node/openvpn/install.sh); the dashboard
gets an OpenVPN card with the profile download, username and password.
Switched off until turned on in the settings.
