Fix: approving a card-to-card request whose order was already paid no longer reports a failure. When the order was paid through a gateway, by the panel, or by a duplicate tap whose write landed between the reads, the request is now closed as approved without granting the package a second time, and the Telegram result page says "the order was already paid; nothing was applied again" instead of "could not complete / this order was already paid another way". A repeated tap on an already-decided request is answered as already handled. Nothing is ever granted twice: the request claim and the order update stay in one transaction.
