Exit location: a New IP button on every server that leaves through a Tor exit. It asks the node's agent (1.3.0, update with digitsell-xray update) to restart that country's Tor node (tor-geo rotate); the row shows the countdown and then the old and the new IP, or why it failed. Users of that country are cut for one to two minutes. Tor picks the IP, so it can give the same one again.
