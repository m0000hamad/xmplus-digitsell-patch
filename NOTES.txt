Fix: approving a subscription or top-up whose package row is gone no longer fails with "the package behind this order is gone". The order keeps a snapshot of what it was sold with (orders.bandwidth, orders.plan, orders.plan_expire), so the grant is rebuilt from the order when the package is missing; the package's routing limits (server_group, iplimit, speedlimit) are left as they are instead of being zeroed. A top-up with no bandwidth snapshot, and a time plan or wallet charge that cannot be identified without its package marker, are marked paid and left to their owning job; the admin sees a note on the request. Reserves are still recognised through plan_reserve and linked directly. Nothing is ever granted twice.
