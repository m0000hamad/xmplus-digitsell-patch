Cancelling a reservation now tells the buyer - on Telegram and by e-mail - that the amount was returned to their account credit and is applied automatically in future purchases (ReserveJob::announceRefund, fired by reserve.cancel right after the refund). The pay-as-you-go wallet also shows its amounts in rial from now on, like the panel wallet (user.money) and the order totals: payg_show_toman defaults to 0 and migration 024 flips the stored setting, so the wallet balance, the charge presets, the minimum charge, every pay-as-you-go price and every message are written in rial, with no /10 toman conversion anywhere. The admin can switch the toman display back on in Settings. Manifest and VERSION to 1.26.0.
