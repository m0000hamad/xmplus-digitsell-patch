The dashboard notices a Telegram gift on its own.

While a gift could still arrive - Telegram not linked yet, the channel card
showing, or the bot-link gift on offer - the page asks
xmplus-patch.php?do=tggift.poll every 15 seconds while the tab is visible,
for up to 30 minutes, and reloads once a gift has landed (the congratulation
popup and the new traffic then show) or the Telegram link changed. Coming
back to the tab, usually from Telegram, asks at once and moves the account to
the front of the next channel check. It never reloads over an open dialog.
