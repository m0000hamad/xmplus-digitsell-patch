Fix: cancelling a reservation sent a Telegram notice only when there was a refund. Now every cancel is told: with a refund the message says the amount was returned to the account credit and is applied automatically, and without a refund (unpaid, zero-amount, or order already settled) a cancel message is sent instead. `ReserveJob::announceCancelled` was added and `reconcile()` no longer skips zero-amount reservations. Manifest and VERSION to 1.26.2.
