Promotion stickers are about 30% smaller, and each promotion now has a
sticker theme: automatic (follows the current season), spring, summer,
autumn, winter, Nowruz, Yalda, Christmas, Mother day, Father day, Girl day,
Boy day or classic. Each theme has its own colours, corner ornament and
falling particles on the card and the plan page banner. Choosing an
occasion theme fills the occasion text if it is empty.
