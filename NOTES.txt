Time plans and traffic top-ups can now each be limited by server group as well
as by subscription plan.

Both pickers sit next to each other in the plan form, and both are open when
left empty - which is what everything already configured is. They are separate
filters and both have to pass: a top-up limited to the VIP plan and to the
Direct group reaches only VIP customers who are also in Direct. The group is
matched against user.server_group.

A time plan keeps its group list in its own marker, alongside applies_to. A
top-up cannot - the encoded /admin/plan/save rewrites order_note on every save -
so it gets a second settings row, timeplan_topup_groups, written at the same
moment as the plan scope.
