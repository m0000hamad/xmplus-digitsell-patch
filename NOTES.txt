Fix: a reserved plan only started once the current plan's time had expired, so a customer whose traffic ran out while days remained (no data but time left) never got their reservation. `ReserveJob::activateDue()` now treats a used-up quota the same as an expired plan - the reservation starts when the current plan's time is up or its traffic is used up. A dated reservation still waits for its date. Manifest and VERSION to 1.26.4.
