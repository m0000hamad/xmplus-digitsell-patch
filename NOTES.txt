Telegram gifts now say more, on Telegram and on the site.

The bot's message states what was won and the plan's total traffic after it,
usable until the plan ends (date and days left). After the bot-link gift it
also points at the channel gift (up to 50 GB) while that is still open.

A congratulation popup on the dashboard (giftpopup.tpl, User::newGifts())
shows each gift not yet seen - amount, reason, total traffic and end date,
the channel nudge - with confetti. Reading it marks it seen (not for an admin
previewing a portal). tgjoin_log and tgbind_log gained a seen column.

A linked account that has not joined yet is asked again every 2 minutes
instead of 10, so the channel card goes soon after an approval.
