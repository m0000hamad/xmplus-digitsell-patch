Plan reservations are now announced everywhere and are impossible to miss. A notice banner sits at the top of every customer page when a reservation is taken and again when it starts (dismissible; reserve.ack marks it seen, table reserve_notice, migration 022); the dashboard carries a fuller reserved-plan card (plan, cycle, days, quota, when it starts, amount paid, cancel); and Telegram plus e-mail go out at both moments, exactly once each (the unique ref on reserve_notice is the guard). A just-paid reservation is also pulled in on the first page load after the gateway (User::planReserveWaiting -> ReserveJob::linkForUser) instead of waiting for the cron, so the card and banner are there the moment the customer returns. Manifest and VERSION to 1.25.0.
