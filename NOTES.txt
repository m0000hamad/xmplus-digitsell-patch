Direct routes for Xray customers on Happ: Filimo, Aparat, Digikala, Telewebion
and other Iranian services that are not under .ir are now on the direct list
when the Iran switch is on, and the subscription also sends the routing-enable
header so Happ applies the profile. It only reaches Happ through the link the
dashboard shows (xmplus-patch.php?do=sub); links imported earlier (/link/...)
need the nginx line from HANDOFF or to be added again.

Pay-as-you-go accounts now use server groups chosen in Settings. Select which
existing groups may keep connecting after a subscription ends, plus a default
group for customers who charge an account without ever buying a subscription.
Accounts on other groups stay disconnected and their wallet is not spent.
