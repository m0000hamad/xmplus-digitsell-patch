Redesigns the notices feature end to end.

User timeline (view/user/notice/notice.tpl): the grey step-rail becomes a stack
of colour-spined cards that finally show notice.title, carry a date pill and a
"new" flag for the last three days, follow dark mode, and have an empty state.

Latest-notice popup (dashboard): gradient header, the title shown above the
body, a scrollable IRANSans body, and the previously unlabelled "don't show
again" switch now reads as one, next to a "Got it" button. The #noticemodal id
and the .modal-check[name=modal-check] the cookie JS binds to are unchanged.

Admin add/edit (view/admin/notices/*.tpl): a two-pane card layout, the TinyMCE
editor set RTL with the panel's own Persian face (IRANSans) for fa_IR, a row of
one-click ready-made snippets (welcome, maintenance, price change, new server,
outage) also wired into the editor's template menu, and a live preview pane
that renders the notice exactly as the user's popup will. The save contract is
untouched — #title #status #sendmail #content (+ id on edit) POST the same
fields to /admin/notice/save.

Adds fourteen localization keys to en_US, fa_IR and zh_CN.
