The admins hear about every new Telegram link and every gift.

TgJoinJob sends a note to the panel's admin chat (telegramchatid, or the
chats listed in tgjoin_admin_chats): who linked the bot and what they got -
or why they got nothing (no running plan yet, no purchase yet, already
gifted) - and who joined the channel and what they won, with the plan's
remaining traffic and end date.
