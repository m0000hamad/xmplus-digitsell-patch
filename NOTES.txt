Invite nudges on the user side, and a one-time gift for joining the Telegram
channel.

Invite popup (dashboard): three messages in turn - the offer, what is lost
without the link, the discount on the next plan - each only after the previous
was dismissed (12 hours, then 2 days, then every 7 days). Any share button
silences it for 30 days. It waits for the notice and commission popups to
close first, opens as a bottom sheet on phones and as two columns on wide
screens. An "assistant" talks through the visitor's own numbers: how many they
invited, how many bought, what it earned, what the ones who have not bought
yet would bring, and the real average commission of one purchase over the last
90 days. The service's features are listed for the friend's sake, and a note
asks to invite friends and acquaintances only, never public channels.

Affiliate page: the same assistant, plus a ready-to-send invite message with
the features and the visitor's own link, editable and remembered per browser,
with Telegram, WhatsApp, system share and copy buttons.

Telegram channel gift: TgJoinJob (bin/tgjoin.php, every minute) asks the Bot
API whether linked accounts have joined the channel. New members only - the
account must be seen outside the channel before it is seen inside; one already
inside at the first look is closed without a gift. Exactly once per site
account and per Telegram account (both UNIQUE in tgjoin_log), so leaving and
rejoining or relinking gets nothing. The gift is a weighted draw: 1-50 GB on a
running plan (about 6.7 GB on average), or a free 10-50 GB plan for 30 days on
package 18 when there is none (about 18 GB on average). The bot tells the
member what they won. Settings: tgjoin_channel_id, tgjoin_link,
tgjoin_free_package, tgjoin_free_days.
