Promotion end date is easier to set:
- a 📅 button opens a Solar Hijri calendar (Saturday first, Fridays in red,
  past days greyed out) with hour and minute, "today" and "no deadline";
- or type a number of days and a time: "2 days from today, until 18:00".
Both fill the end-date field; it is still shown in words underneath and
counted on Tehran time.
