Charge wallet (pay-as-you-go), switched off until you turn it on in
Settings → کیف پول شارژ و پورسانت.

Customers top up any amount from 200,000 toman (tiered bonus, e.g. +5% from
1,000,000). When their plan runs out - data or time - they stay connected and
usage is taken from the balance at each server's price per GB, like a SIM
card; nothing is billed while a server is down. They are told on the
dashboard, Telegram and e-mail when the plan is ending, when billing starts,
when the balance is low or gone, and when a top-up lands. Buying a plan stops
the billing.

Admin: price per GB per server, totals of charged / spent / unspent, every
wallet with its ledger, manual adjustment. Commission can be kept in its own
wallet that only pays for subscription plans; a one-time button moves the
current panel wallets into it.
